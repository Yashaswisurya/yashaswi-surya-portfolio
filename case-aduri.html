Case Study

Aduri Group

Meta-led lead generation and direct sales support across multiple campaign efforts.

All case studies

Challenge

Support real-estate acquisition with a lead generation model that could qualify interest and support downstream sales follow-up.

Strategy

Develop a multi-campaign Meta strategy built around lead quality, audience relevance and appropriate follow-up motion for plot sales opportunities.

Execution

  • Three Meta campaigns structured for lead flow and qualification.
  • Messaging and audience refinement to align with plot sales interest.
  • Campaign optimization for higher-intent enquiry quality.
