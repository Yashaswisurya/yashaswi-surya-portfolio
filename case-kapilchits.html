Challenge

Generate qualified leads at efficient cost while supporting a client funnel that could translate inquiry interest into business conversion value.

Strategy

Focus on efficient acquisition with a lead-quality lens, making sure campaign activity was aligned with a higher-intent prospect journey and conversion follow-up process.

Execution

  • Targeted digital acquisition strategy.
  • Lead capture and qualification framework.
  • Campaign optimization aligned to CPL efficiency and conversion quality.
