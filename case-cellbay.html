01 / THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

Digital attention needed to create meaningful product interest and local demand across a 50+ location retail network.

The starting point called for more than posting more often: the social channel, acquisition setup and location coverage needed to work toward connected store and website journeys.

04 / WHAT I BUILT

What I built

My contribution connected creator activity, content, paid acquisition and retail conversion into a more coordinated growth system.

01

CREATOR-LED ACQUISITION

Built and coordinated a regional creator program using influencers, local meme pages and food bloggers to create location-specific demand.

02

CONTENT ENGINE

Developed hooks, scripts, offer structures and recurring content formats, contributing to 1,000+ reels.

03

PAID ACQUISITION

Managed Meta acquisition activity that generated 17K+ documented leads across the available campaign accounts.

04

RETAIL CONVERSION LOOP

Connected social activity with DMs, calls, CRM reporting, website orders and store/product-level sales tracking.

05

BRAND & PRODUCT CAMPAIGNS

Worked with device brands and their sales/marketing teams on product promotions, launches and retail demand-generation campaigns.
