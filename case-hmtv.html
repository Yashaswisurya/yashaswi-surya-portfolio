01 / THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

HMTV already had a large YouTube audience and a high publishing volume. The opportunity was to structure the operation more deliberately around monetization and content performance.

  • Heavy dependence on AdSense revenue.
  • Long-form stories were not consistently structured for stronger YouTube performance.
  • Stories could be split into additional content opportunities.
  • Shorts were not being used as systematically as they could be.
  • Analytics and content-performance signals needed a larger role in planning.
  • Entertainment and interview opportunities were underused from a monetization perspective.

04 / WHAT I BUILT

What I built

01

YOUTUBE CONTENT STRATEGY

Structured long-form content around 4–6 minute formats and identified opportunities to split stories into additional content.

02

SHORTS DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM

Used long-form stories as sources for multiple Shorts and distributed short-form content across YouTube and Instagram.

03

CONTENT PACKAGING

Optimized titles, thumbnails and hooks, regularly using vidIQ scores and YouTube Studio performance signals.

04

HIGH-VOLUME WORKFLOW

Worked within a high-volume publishing operation, coordinating content planning, approval and workflow across multiple editorial categories.

05

MONETIZATION STRATEGY

Focused the operation more strongly on views, watch hours, content volume and monetization opportunities to increase YouTube revenue.
